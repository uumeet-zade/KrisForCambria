First Rally in Fayre, Cambria

Taking the message to the heart of rural Caprica.

Return to Home

Dalgaard's first rally was announced approximately a week in advance of it happening, in Fayre Park, the primary public space of the regional area of Fayre. Since their loss in the 2060 election for Reno, Dalgaard had wanted to articulate their thoughts, creating clear-cut policies, and get the rural vote on their side.

The location was chosen by Dalgaard themselves due to their love of the environment and nature, showing how they wanted to preserve the environment of rural places. The park was also nearby to many local businesses, and it was also large enough to accommodate large crowds of people who would want to show up. Nonetheless, Dalgaard's train from Alto arrived in Fayre as they tapped off and walked to the gardens casually. They came with a small crew of assistants, as most people assisting with the rally were getting it all set up in the primary public area of the park. Casually, Dalgaard brushed themselves off and walked up onto the stage, waving out to the people who did show up.

"My fellow Cambrians! It truly is wonderful to see you and thank you for showing up today. My friends, look up and down the streets leading here. We here in Fayre are lucky, as local business is what keeps us going, not just as a town but as a close-knit community. This is one of the matters in which I want to discuss here today, being the protection of our locally owned and sourced businesses.

This is why, if elected, I will push a series of tax cuts for new businesses employing locally. Local businesses are, often, what gives a town like this our identity. Because we, as people, are strong. We have dreams and we take action, building upon them. But for many, this is often hard, as big corporations drive locals out of business due to the act of predatory pricing. This is why, if elected to parliament, I will call to bring an end to predatory pricing and the monopoly which has plagued our businesses for far too long. My friends, I call upon you to keep fighting to support local business. These multinationals trying to drive our good friends out of business has gone on for far too long, and we need to all collectively do something about it.

This is also why I call upon our most essential public services, such as hospitals and schools, to source their materials from local sources, and to promote our local services. I have been passionate since the beginning of my political career about a fight-back against the predator multinationals driving people out of business. I cannot allow this to happen for any longer.

Now, my friends, my opponent is one Pepe Marti Rutte. I, of course do wish the best of luck to him in this election, yet I cannot help but believe he's a bit unfit for the job. Just recently, he made a Caprik post stating that he is on the road to recovery and building a new path of my life. And yes, no more politics. Ever. Now this promise of no more politics ever was immediately backflipped on this election. This election, I'm coming up against an opponent who cannot even decide if he wants to serve the people? Politics is something that you need to commit to. How can we trust that he's ready to serve the people? The field of politics is not something you can just turn up and do, you need to be actively in the mindset of I am not here to serve my own interests, I am here to give my people a voice.

That is the democracy that we live in here in Caprica, and it is the mindset that all politicians need to be in if they truly believe they are fit to govern. My opponent's Caprik statements prove that he, simply, is incapable of serving the people of Cambria, and of Caprica as a whole. He is not to be trusted.

Now, I am giving this speech in a regional city, and I believe that regional and rural areas would heavily benefit with more connections towards the major cities, which is why if elected, I will endorse and fight to deliver improvements in order to develop rural infrastructure nodes for essentials and for transport, in order to connect our rural areas to the big cities through a regional population spot. Transport interchanges, connections to important assets, these things must be spread to our whole community.

And now I know many in these rural areas who will be getting these assets are going to be using it to improve their farmland. Now something about this farmland, my friends, is that multinational corporations keep desperately trying to claw at it and take it away to better their own interests. However, I say no to that! I will always fight for your right to manage your own land. And to those who say, This doesn't affect me, why should I care?, imagine if your home was swept up and forced to be vacated because a large corporation wanted to build a new project on that land? It's unacceptable, and it will not stand!

So, I call upon you, my friends, to vote for true change and keep Cambria red! Be the change you want to see in government! The tides are turning, so bring on the red wave! Thank you all for attending!"

Dalgaard's team walked onto stage, and they answered a few questions crowd members had as they walked off. Soon after, they walked back to the station and to their residence in Alto.

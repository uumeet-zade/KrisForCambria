Second Rally in Alto, Cambria

A powerful gathering calling for workers' rights and collective action.

Return to Home

Unlike their previous rally in Fayre, this rally in Alto was publicised for about two to three weeks before it actually happened, with Dalgaard aiming to get as many motivated people there as possible and encouraging undecided voters to attend too. This rally was longer and more organised, with guest speakers from union leaders to left-wing activists, even ordinary people who called for change in politics. After that, however, Dalgaard took the stage to resounding applause as they calmed the crowd down.

"Greetings, everyone! Whenever I have one of these rallies, it always puts a smile on my face to see so many different faces in the crowd. People young and old, people of all different backgrounds and cultures, all united under the banner of one cause. Now, I'm here today because I want to deliver the policies that the masses want to see, and I am here to be your voice to parliament, always and forever willing.

Now, I did not enter politics to boost my wage, or to gain a position of power over others, but because I stand here as a willing potential public servant. My opponent recently made statements on Caprik in which he said he would not enter the field of politics ever. Now, clearly that was a lie, because he's running against me. And so that begs the question, how can we trust Pepe Marti Rutte when he's indecisive on his own choice to be a candidate? How do we know he's fit to serve the people, and not just doing this to gain power?

It also begs the question, who does Rutte really stand for? I do my job because I want to give the masses a voice in parliament. The small business owners, the unionists, the people of rural Caprica. Our country needs change when we're being stared in the face by privatisation meant to bolster the profits of the already-rich instead of nationalising services and putting them under public ownership made to serve the people.

The rich and multinational companies need representation of the workers as a voice. This is why if elected, I will call upon a mandate for union representation on the board of directors for various companies. And this way, the bad wages and working conditions will not go without opposition, giving our workers the voice which they have been trying to get for so long. This is why I also want to assure and reaffirm my commitment to the protection of collective bargaining, so workers are able to get the wages that they need and deserve to live for the hard work they do, alongside the right to call out injustices in our workplace in regard to the working conditions themselves.

Truly, we need to call it out where we all see fit, so our workers can remain hard at work and provide the resources necessary for us to simply call Caprica home. Now, when these injustices get out of hand, it is only natural for the workers to strike in order to call it out. I am a firm believer that this right needs to be protected. Workers have a right to their own freedom of speech, just as we all do here in Caprica. This is why I will always and forever fight to preserve the workers' and unions' right to strike against the injustices presented upon them by their government or other higher board. Without freedom of speech and freedom of expression, we are blatantly doing a disservice to our future generations who will see injustice and believe there's nothing wrong with it. It's a disgrace!

Now lastly for today, I would like to give my biggest thanks to our troops and the people that keep our wonderful nation protected. Truly, they are the heroes that we should cheer for, not the people running government. We wouldn't even have a country without our military, and for the service they do, they deserve to be repaid adequately. This is why, alongside trying to push these services to the public, we will fight to provide affordable higher education and public housing to returned servicemen and women. They help keep our country as safe and democratic as it is, and they deserve to get this treatment and to live a fulfilling life because of it.

I will never stop or be quiet in my support for the working-class, and the military fights to keep our country great. With that, friends, I conclude my speech for today. I call upon you all to spread the word of Kris Dalgaard, and to get as many votes as you possibly can. Don't just do it for me, do it for our future, and our people. Thank you all for attending, and have a wonderful afternoon!"

Dalgaard walked away from the podium and back towards their car, answering questions about policy for supporters along the way. They waved as they drove off, calling upon the crowd one last time to "never lose hope".

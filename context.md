# Context: 2064 Constituency Election in Cambria, Caprica

## General Information
- **Election:** 2064 Constituency election
- **Location:** Cambria, Caprica
- **Candidate:** Kris Dalgaard
- **Party:** Social Democratic Alliance (SDA)
- **Opponent:** Pepe Marti Rutte
- **Theme Color:** Rose Red
- **Party Logo:** `SDAlogo.svg` (located in the workspace)

## Candidate Profile: Kris Dalgaard
Kris Dalgaard is a passionate, environmentally-conscious, and left-leaning candidate who strongly advocates for the working class, local businesses, and rural communities. Following a loss in the 2060 election for Reno, Dalgaard has refined their policies to specifically target the needs of the masses and the rural voters of Cambria (such as Fayre and Alto). Dalgaard positions themselves as a dedicated public servant, contrasting with their opponent Pepe Marti Rutte, whom Dalgaard criticizes as indecisive and disconnected from the people (due to past statements on "Caprik" about quitting politics).

## Key Policies and Platform

### Local Business & Economy
- Implement a series of tax cuts for new businesses that employ locally.
- Take strong action to end predatory pricing and dismantle monopolies created by multinational corporations.
- Mandate that essential public services (hospitals, schools) source their materials from local sources and promote local services.

### Rural Development & Environment
- Develop rural infrastructure nodes for essentials and transport to better connect regional/rural areas to major cities.
- Protect rural farmland from being taken over by multinational corporations, ensuring locals can manage their own land.
- Preserve the environment and nature of rural places.

### Workers' Rights & Unions
- Mandate union representation on the board of directors for various companies to ensure fair wages and working conditions.
- Protect collective bargaining rights to ensure workers receive the wages they need and deserve.
- Defend the right of workers and unions to strike against injustices and preserve freedom of speech in the workplace.

### Public Services & Military Veterans
- Oppose privatization; support nationalizing services and putting them under public ownership.
- Provide affordable higher education and public housing to returned servicemen and women in recognition of their service.

A Voice for the Masses

In the heart of Cambria, where close knit communities like Fayre and Alto thrive, a movement is taking root. Kris Dalgaard understands the fragile balance between our natural environment and the local businesses that keep our towns alive. For too long, multinational corporations have dictated our future, leaving ordinary people behind. Kris stepped into the political arena to change the story.

True public service demands unwavering dedication. While others treat politics as a fleeting interest or a backup plan, Kris has consistently fought for the working class with a clear vision and a steady hand. It is time for a representative who stands firmly with the people of Caprica and brings real, passionate leadership to parliament.

Key Policies

Real solutions for a stronger Cambria.

💼

Local Business & Economy

Our towns lose their soul when local shops close their doors. We plan to introduce tax cuts specifically for new businesses that hire from within our community. Predatory pricing by monopolies must end, and our essential public services like schools and hospitals will be required to source their materials locally. We are building an economy that stays in Cambria.

🌾

Rural Development

The land we live on shapes who we are. We are committed to developing essential infrastructure nodes that connect our rural areas directly to major cities, ensuring no community is left isolated. Furthermore, we will fiercely defend our farmland from corporate takeover, allowing families to manage the land they know and love.

✊

Workers' Rights & Unions

Hard work deserves fair representation. We will mandate that unions have a seat on the board of directors across various companies. We remain deeply committed to protecting collective bargaining. When workplaces fail their employees, the fundamental right to strike must be protected and preserved as a basic freedom of speech.

🏥

Public Services

A society is judged by how it cares for its people. We stand firmly against the privatization of our essential services, pushing to bring them back under public ownership. Our military personnel have sacrificed for our safety, and they deserve affordable higher education and public housing upon their return.
